## 🎓 Teaching Philosophy

My teaching approach is built on several core principles:

### 1. Learning by Doing
Students learn best through hands-on practice. Each session includes:
- Live coding demonstrations
- Guided exercises with increasing difficulty
- Real-world project implementations
- Debugging sessions and troubleshooting

### 2. Bridging Theory and Practice
- Start with intuitive explanations before mathematical formulations
- Provide real-world context for every concept
- Connect theoretical knowledge to practical applications
- Use medical imaging and healthcare examples when relevant

### 3. Adaptability and Support
- Adjust pace based on student comprehension
- Provide additional resources for struggling students
- Offer advanced challenges for quick learners
- Maintain open communication channels for questions

### 4. Industry-Relevant Skills
- Teach tools and practices used in professional settings
- Focus on reproducible and well-documented code
- Emphasize collaborative development workflows
- Prepare students for research and industry roles
